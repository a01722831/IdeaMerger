Competitor names used in kill reasons are known incumbents; where they were not re-searched in this project the reason says so, and every **developed** seed gets a full competition check in Phase 4.

Status legend:
- **developed**: advanced to Phase 4 (concept note below, then a full idea file).
- **merged into X**: folded into a stronger seed as a feature or segment.
- **killed: reason**: fails a red zone or kill criterion (reason given).
- **pending**: waiting for evidence from a running research track (resolved before Phase 4).

## Advanced seeds: concept notes
Each note: problem, product, insight, signals. The number is the idea file (`ideas/NNN-slug.md`); the letter is the development batch. Notes are deliberately sharp so the development pass can test them, not just restate them.

**001 Haul Paid (S001, batch B).** Problem: owner-operators get short-paid $100-375 on $1,000 loads and refused the detention and layover pay that rate confirmations promise, and lack time to fight (PRO-029, PRO-030); small brokers burn 45 minutes a day on check calls (PRO-026). Product: forward rate cons, BOLs, lumper receipts and ELD or GPS exports; AI builds a per-load claim (geofence timestamps vs appointment times vs contract clauses), sends escalating demands to broker AP, and if unpaid prepares a surety-bond (BMC-84) claim or FMCSA complaint; contingency pricing. Insight: the evidence already sits in the driver's phone and inbox; what is missing is assembling it in broker-AP language and escalating to the bond, which brokers fear. Also GAP-20: a $650 detention claim rejected for a missing signed BOL time and a missed two-hour notice email, which is exactly the evidence trail this product keeps. Signals: PRO-029, PRO-030, PRO-026, REV-35, GAP-20.

**002 Shoebox (S002, batch C).** Problem: HSA holders who pay now and reimburse later must keep readable receipts for decades; paper fades, folders rot, and substantiating $50K means scanning hundreds of bills (LIFE-09, LIFE-10, LIFE-11). Product: forward or photograph receipts, EOBs and pharmacy statements; AI extracts and matches bill to EOB to prove out-of-pocket cost, checks eligibility, de-duplicates, and keeps an audit-ready ledger of the unreimbursed balance. Insight: the valuable artifact is the matched bill-plus-EOB pair, which custodians' receipt vaults do not produce. Signals: LIFE-09, LIFE-10, LIFE-11.

**003 Fix Desk (S003, batch B).** Problem: self-managing landlords live in phone tag between tenants and plumbers but refuse to pay a manager 10% of rent for dispatch (PRO-011); small teams get requests on personal numbers and WhatsApp (PRO-012); tenants and owners dislike incumbents' AI front doors (REV-13, REV-14, REV-15). Product: one text, WhatsApp and voice line per portfolio; AI triages with photos and troubleshooting, applies the owner's urgency rules, books the owner's own vendors by text, confirms access windows, follows up and logs cost per unit; owner approves with one tap; compliance-notice module (PRO-013). Insight: owners want their own vendors and approval control; the AI should coordinate three parties and escalate, not speak for the owner. Signals: PRO-011, PRO-012, PRO-013, REV-13, REV-14, REV-15.

**004 Scope Guard (S004, batch B).** Problem: remodelers give away extras because clients treat on-site requests as in scope and resist change orders (PRO-009); owners do admin at night (PRO-006, RED-39). Product: watches the contractor's texts, WhatsApp and voice memos, detects scope changes against the signed estimate, drafts a priced change order from the contractor's rates, and sends a one-tap e-signature before work proceeds; running extras ledger for the final invoice. Insight: the change order dies at the moment of the request; capturing it in the message thread changes behavior more than any project-management feature. Signals: PRO-009, PRO-006, RED-39, PRO-031.

**005 Grant Ledger (S005, batch A).** Problem: small nonprofits cannot say which grant paid for an expense and spend days reconstructing it (PRO-208); funder and board reports are built by hand (PRO-211, PRO-212); data work goes to $10-15K consultants (PRO-209). Product: connects to QuickBooks or Xero and the bank, reads each grant agreement (allowable costs, budget lines, periods, restrictions), allocates every expense with an evidence trail, and drafts funder reports, budget-vs-actuals and board packs; warns about unspent or disallowed costs before deadlines.
