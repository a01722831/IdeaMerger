# Landscape A: AI launches (May 2025 to Oct 2026)

Owner: subagent, 2026-10-31. Signal prefix: LAND.

Goal: map what AI products are launching, what is crowded, what is emerging, and collect raw material for remixing. Calibration point: HeyClicky (YC Spring 2026) and the "AI lives where the work happens" family.

Reading conventions used below:
- "PH mirror" = a hunted.space dashboard page (Product Hunt is blocked). "monthly #N" = Product Hunt's own monthly rank as mirrored by hunted.space; votes and comments are the numbers that site displays. "PH daily mirror" = aitoolly.com copy of a Product Hunt daily leaderboard.
- YC batch codes: W26 = Winter 2026, P26 = Spring 2026 (HeyClicky's YC page says "P26 / Spring 2026"), S26 = Summer 2026, F26 = Fall 2026.
- "(tagline only)" = I saw only the one-line pitch, nothing deeper. "[UNVERIFIED]" = claim I could not confirm on a primary page. "[interpretation]" = my reading, not a sourced fact.
- Counts marked "my regex" are keyword counts I computed over page text. They are approximate and good for direction, not exact shares.

## Method

Sources that worked and how they were read:
- **YC directory data**: the public mirror `https://yc-oss.github.io/api/` (`meta.json` says last updated 2026-10-31T03:22Z), one JSON file per batch for Winter 2025 to Fall 2026 (batch sizes 165, 144, 166, 145, 197, 193, 228, 197). Seven live YC company pages (heyclicky, memoir, jo, sentient-os, minicor, screenpipe, baseframe) were fetched directly and matched the mirror on batch, one-liner and description. YC-published batch-level statistics: none found.
- **Product Hunt**: producthunt.com list pages return 403, so I used (a) hunted.space monthly rankings for March, June, September and October 2026 (50 products each) plus its history page, (b) aitoolly.com daily leaderboards for 2025-06-03, 2025-07-15, 2025-10-14, 2025-11-11, 2026-02-10 and 2026-10-23, (c) three Product Hunt community posts that WebFetch could read, (d) search snippets. Hunted.space had no official ranking for the three 2025 months I sampled (June, September, December: "No official ranking is available for this month yet"), so 2025 Product Hunt coverage is thin.
- **Other launch sites** (read with curl, parsed with Python): BetaList (home pages 1 to 3 = 141 unique startups listed 2026-10-23 to 2026-10-31, plus three AI topic pages), Uneed (home page, 24 launches listed for 2026-10-31), Microlaunch (home page, five category pages, four product pages), Futurepedia (`/ai-tools` category counts).
- **Hacker News** (Algolia API via `scripts/hn_search.py`): Launch HN list since 2026-04-01, Show HN searches for Mac voice and overlay assistants, the Screenpipe Launch HN thread, comment searches for "heyclicky", and single-post lookups for Atticus, VibeWhisper, Wisp, Overture and Nowdex.
- **Product sites read directly**: heyclicky.com, the farzaa/clicky README (raw GitHub), voiceos.com, highlightai.com, littlebird.ai, tryclico.com, usecarly.com.
- **WebFetch (10 reads)**: timewell.jp (YC Summer 2026 analysis), extruct.ai (YC W26 analysis), TechCrunch (Cluely, 2025-07-03), OpenAI Developer Community (ChatGPT Voice on desktop), dealroom.co/yc-batches, three Product Hunt community posts, plus one 403 each on peerlist.io and theresanaiforthat.com.
- **WebSearch (24 queries)**: (1) HeyClicky Clicky YC Spring 2026 Mac app cursor sees your screen voice; (2) Product Hunt Golden Kitty Awards 2025 winners AI products; (3) Product Hunt "Product of the Day" AI agent launch October 2026; (4) best Product Hunt launches 2026 AI products roundup top upvoted; (5) YC Spring 2026 demo day AI startups batch trends; (6) HeyClicky alternatives screen-aware AI desktop assistant Cluely Highlight AI Clico Mac 2026; (7) voice-first desktop agent control computer with voice launch 2026 Product Hunt OR Show HN; (8) proactive AI assistant that watches your screen and offers help without asking launch 2026; (9) Farza Clicky AI teacher next to your cursor viral demo April 2026 Buildspace founder; (10) hunted.space clicky 2.0 Product Hunt upvotes HeyClicky; (11) Product Hunt Orbit Awards winners coding agents AI meeting note-takers vibecoding 2025 2026; (12) Product Hunt top products of 2025 year in review AI most upvoted launches; (13) Cluely funding valuation ARR 2026 Roy Lee screen overlay AI; (14) Peerlist Launchpad weekly top projects AI tools 2026; (15) "There's An AI For That" number of AI tools listed 2026; (16) Toolify.ai total number of AI tools listed 2026 directory; (17) Garry Tan Y Combinator batch percent AI companies Summer 2026 Fall 2026 batch size agents; (18) OpenAI ChatGPT Voice desktop app control computer GPT-Live July 2026 announcement; (19) Goldfish "Press Option" AI replies like you knows your work Product Hunt June 2026; (20) Claude Duet proactive agent understands what needs your attention Anthropic Product Hunt October 2026; (21) Peerlist Launchpad project of the week AI agent (peerlist.io only); (22) TechCrunch Y Combinator Spring 2026 Demo Day standout startups HeyClicky; (23) Cluely Roy Lee admits ARR claim March 2026 TechCrunch (returned unrelated results); (24) Wispr Flow funding round 2026 voice dictation users valuation.
- **Request volume**: about 90 requests to static pages and public JSON files (many are one-file-per-batch or one-page-per-month mirrors) and 12 HN API calls. That is above the brief's rough 30-call guide; no single site was hit repeatedly, and failures were not retried more than once or twice.

Definitions behind "my regex" counts: "AI-ish words" = AI, LLM, machine learning, generative, copilot, GPT, language model, agent(s), agentic, foundation model, autonomous (searched in one-liner plus long description). "agent*" = agent(s), agentic, autonomous (one-liner only). Robotics = robot, humanoid, drone, physical AI. Sales/GTM = SDR, outbound, cold email, sales, GTM, lead(s). Support = customer support/service, helpdesk. Hand counts are labelled "hand count".

Deliverable counts: 95 rows in the products table, 11 demand signals, 24 searches, 10 fetches.

## Emerging categories

## Interaction patterns worth stealing

1. **Consent by gesture: capture only while a key is pressed or held.** HeyClicky sees the screen only on the hotkey and says screenshots are not stored; Goldfish triggers on Option; Atticus, Ida and Incredible are hold-to-talk. Always-on capture (Screenpipe, Moonshot) drew the sharpest privacy objections in my sample (LAND-01, LAND-02).
2. **Cursor as context and pointer.** VoiceOS ("Your cursor is the context"), Clicky's launch feature "Points directly at UI elements", Open Portal's box-select, Agentation's visual feedback for agents. HeyClicky's site now says it "draws right on your screen to point the way". An HN post groups Agentation, Claude Desktop, Codex, Roughdraft and heyclicky as "spatial feedback loops" ([HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48925653)).
3. **Meter the expensive thing, not the conversation.** HeyClicky: talk is unlimited on paid tiers, agent messages are capped (25, 150, 1,000 a month) and "talk keeps working, always" when the agent cap is hit.
4. **Ride the user's existing model access.** Atticus runs hard tasks through `claude -p` on a Claude Pro/Max plan, Turnstone fails over between the user's subscriptions, VibeWhisper takes your own Whisper key, Agibot is "free forever with your key", OpenWork supports 50+ providers. Cost of goods moves to the user; dependence on vendor terms is the risk [interpretation].
5. **Cheap path first: local routing, replay and show-once.** Atticus handles simple commands locally in about 0.08 s and replays saved steps without AI (30 s first run, 5 to 8 s after); Minicor sells "self-healing" automations behind an endpoint; IrisGo ("show it once, let it run"); Screenpipe turns recorded work into agents; BaseFrame mines existing history instead of recording.
6. **Autonomy with explicit approval gates.** Dardaris ("you just approve"), UstaWork ("gates you approve"), Actionbox, Claude Duet (consent before payments or signing, secondary coverage), Altair Health (a theorem prover checks each action, failures go to a human biller).
7. **The agent lives in a channel people already use.** Karpo (iMessage), Chert (iMessage as infrastructure), [MakersClaw](https://hunted.space/dashboard/makersclaw) ("AI employees that live in your Slack, Teams, Telegram"), [Switch](https://hunted.space/dashboard/switch-11) (any agent into Slack, Teams and Discord), [Carly](https://www.usecarly.com/) ("Email Carly to create AI agents"), [Novu Connect](https://hunted.space/dashboard/novu) ("Ship agents where your users already work").
8. **Background and overnight work.** Sentient OS runs nightly on-device inference so work is "already prepared" in the morning; HeyClicky's "HeyClicky Agent" runs tasks in the background; Agent 37 keeps sandboxes alive and bills only active minutes.
9. **One orchestrator to talk to, plus a phone remote.** Rindler's orchestrator reports what landed, what it decided for you and what needs your call; Paradev and Cursor for iOS add phones; Muster flags the blocked agent; Nowdex puts quota on the Lock Screen.
10. **Outcome, milestone or per-call pricing.** Panacea (payment due when milestones complete), Orthogonal (providers paid every time an agent calls), Agent 37 (pay for active compute minutes), Altair Health (paid when practices get paid), [convert.express](https://betalist.com/startups/convert-express) (BetaList: "Pay-per-minute AI transcription for audio and video files, no subscription").
11. **Deliver the finished artifact, not a chat answer.** Open Portal ("a finished thumbnail, ad, or redesign"), Arden (audit-ready workpapers), Gauth (whiteboard instead of chat thread), Thesys Agent Builder (UI instead of text), Memoir (the deck appears, the diagram draws itself).
12. **Open-source seed, paid product on top.** Clicky (MIT) went viral, then HeyClicky closed new code (README note 2026-04-27) and sells Pro and Max; community ports followed. Agent Relay (hosted free or self-hosted), Wisp, Hitoku Draft and OpenWork use similar open or hosted splits.
