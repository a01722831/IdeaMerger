# IdeaMerger: startup discovery for a solo, AI-native founder

This repo is a research report, not a product. An AI researcher working alone looked for problems worth solving and invented LLM-native product ideas for a solo founder: an MVP in 4 to 8 weeks, under about $200 a month to run, no team, license, hardware or funding needed to launch. Every pain claim links to a source with a date and an exact quote.

**Bottom line.** 571 sourced pain signals, 216 screened seeds and 41 competition-checked idea files later, 16 ideas are live and none scores above 64 out of 100. Generic small-business AI is saturated in late 2026 (25 of 41 developed ideas died as crowded). What survives are long-lived case files where a family or tiny operator faces a slow institution and today's alternative is a human service costing thousands. Start with [REPORT.md](REPORT.md).

## Top 10
Scores are out of 100 (rubric in [METHODOLOGY.md](METHODOLOGY.md)); differences under about 3 points are noise. Ranks 9 and 10 were picked from a three-way tie for diversity (see the report).

| # | Idea | One-liner | Score | Competition | Form factor |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Medicaid Ready](ideas/006-medicaid-ready.md) | Builds a nursing-home Medicaid application packet from five years of statements, with flagged transfers and drafted letters | 63.2 | Weak direct | Web app |
| 2 | [Contents Claim Builder](ideas/039-contents-claim-builder.md) | Rebuilds an insurer-ready contents inventory from old photos, emails and card exports after a fire or flood | 61.1 | Weak direct | Web app |
| 3 | [Grant Ledger](ideas/005-grant-ledger.md) | Allocates every nonprofit expense to the right grant from the award agreement and drafts funder reports | 60.0 | Weak direct | Web app on QuickBooks or Xero |
| 4 | [Benefits Portal Pilot](ideas/027-benefits-portal-pilot.md) | Side panel that guides navigators through state benefits portals and builds Medicaid work-requirement proof packets | 58.9 | Adjacent only | Browser extension |
| 5 | [Parent Mail Desk](ideas/007-parent-mail-desk.md) | A parent texts a photo of any letter; AI explains it and gives the adult child a one-tap action | 56.8 | Weak direct | SMS plus web |
| 6 | [Haul Paid](ideas/001-haul-paid.md) | Turns rate cons, BOLs and ELD logs into escalating detention and short-pay claims for small carriers | 55.8 | Weak direct | Mobile-friendly web app |
| 7 | [Paper Trail](ideas/008-paper-trail.md) | Photograph an IRS or SSA notice; get its meaning, a drafted reply, tracked certified mail and escalation | 53.7 | Weak direct | Web app plus mail |
| 8 | [Viva Voce](ideas/018-viva-voce.md) | A short realtime voice conversation checks that a student understands the work they submitted | 53.7 | Weak direct | Voice web app |
| 9 | [Do It With Me](ideas/036-do-it-with-me.md) | A family-configured voice guide walks an older parent through bank and portal tasks and refuses scam-like steps | 52.6 | Weak direct | Voice guide |
| 10 | [Chat-to-Ledger](ideas/033-chat-to-ledger.md) | WhatsApp-first micro-sellers forward orders and receipts; AI keeps the ledger and matches payments | 52.6 | Weak direct | Chat-based |

The first eight and Do It With Me have a deep dive (competitor table with prices, bottom-up sizing, unit economics, a first-100-customers plan, a one-week validation test under $50, a paste-ready build brief) and a red-team critique with responses.
