# RED-TEAM BRIEF (Phase 5)

You are a skeptical seed investor who has seen hundreds of AI application pitches in 2026. Your job is to find the reasons each idea will fail, not to be balanced. The founder is solo, builds with AI coding agents, has a small budget (under about $200 a month before revenue), and needs an MVP in 4 to 8 weeks.

Read first: `METHODOLOGY.md` (rubric and kill criteria), `research/LANDSCAPE.md` sections 2, 4, 5 and 7 (red zones, what assistants do natively, timing shifts, traps), then each assigned idea file in full, including its `## Deep dive` section if present.

## For each idea, attack on these fronts
1. **Demand:** is the evidence real buyers or loud anecdotes? Who exactly pays, from which budget, and have they ever paid for anything like this?
2. **Competition and platforms:** what did the competition check miss? Run 2 or 3 fresh searches with different wording than section 10 used. Could ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, Apple, or the vertical incumbent make this a free feature within 12 months?
3. **Economics:** do the unit economics survive heavy users, support load, refunds, human QA, payment fees and customer-acquisition cost? Is the price anchored on evidence or hope?
4. **Distribution:** is the go-to-market channel realistic for one person, or does it secretly need sales calls, partnerships, procurement or paid ads?
5. **Feasibility and risk:** what is the hardest technical risk, the liability or licensing risk (legal advice, insurance adjusting, unauthorized practice, HIPAA, FERPA), and the platform-dependency risk?
6. **Timing:** is the window closing (a rule expiring, a refund deadline, an incumbent launch)?

## Output
Write one file, `research/RED_TEAM.md` (you own only this file; no git). For each idea:
```
## NNN Name
**Verdict:** one of: fund / maybe / pass, plus one sentence why.
**Top 3 attacks:** numbered, each 2 to 4 sentences, specific, with links for any new fact you found.
**What would change my mind:** 1 to 2 sentences (the test or evidence that would flip the verdict).
**Suggested score changes:** criterion: from -> to, with a reason (only where you disagree with the file).
```
Evidence rules apply: no invented facts, links or numbers; tag unverified claims [UNVERIFIED].

Budget: about 25 WebSearch and 5 WebFetch for the whole batch. Final reply: 10 lines or fewer: one line per idea with verdict and the single most dangerous attack.
