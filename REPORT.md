## 4. Landscape
Full synthesis: [research/LANDSCAPE.md](research/LANDSCAPE.md). The short version:
- **The big assistants absorbed the horizontal layer in 2026.** ChatGPT Work, Claude Cowork and Claude Duet, Gemini tasks, Copilot Vision and Autopilot now browse, act, remember, make docs, see the screen and run proactively. OpenAI even retired its own standalone browser and Sora. Generic agents, overlays, memory layers and doc generators are red zones.
- **Small models got very cheap.** Claude Haiku 5.5 and GPT-6 Luna cost $0.10 / $0.50 per 1M tokens, so a typical 3k-in / 500-out call costs about $0.00055. Realtime voice is about $0.05/min. "Process everything" back-office products and voice agents now have workable margins.
- **Investor consensus is crowded.** "Sell outcomes, not tools" in insurance, accounting, healthcare revenue cycle and legal is the most crowded thesis, and those markets need licenses or enterprise sales. YC's newest requests moved to bottlenecks: credentials ("The $15-an-Hour Expert"), government paperwork, aging population, proving you're human, compliance.
- **HeyClicky shows the interaction bar and the risk.** AI that lives by the cursor, sees the screen on a hotkey and talks you through tasks got strong distribution, then was cloned six times and absorbed by labs within months. The defensible version ties that interaction to a narrow job, domain data or a niche system of record.
- **Graveyard lessons:** thin wrappers, features that could be a button in the assistant, hardware, consumer novelty without retention, model cost above price, single-supplier dependency, and selling answers instead of outcomes.
